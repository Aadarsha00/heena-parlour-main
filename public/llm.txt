## Common questions

### How do I book?

Open the Services page, select a service, sign in, and choose an available
date and time.

### Does the website accept payment?

No. The website does not accept online payment.

### How do I change or cancel an appointment?

Sign in and open My Appointments. The options shown there depend on the
appointment's current status and timing.

### How do I contact the salon?

Call (443) 574-4362, email info@beautifulbrowsandhenna.com, or use the contact
page at https://beautifulbrowsandhenna.com/contact.
